# 1508 — Migrate runner-terminal from alacritty_terminal to libghostty-vt

Tracking issue: [#1508](https://github.com/yicheng47/runner/issues/1508). No new UI chrome and no Pencil screen. Phase 1 replaces Runner's VT emulation engine (`alacritty_terminal` 0.26 / `vte` 0.15) with Ghostty's `libghostty-vt` (`vt-1.0.2`, MIT) across `runner-terminal`, `runner-daemon` and `runner-app`; Phase 2 builds Kitty graphics, local binary pixel handoff, pixel geometry, SGR-Pixels mouse and 30 fps GPUI rendering directly on top of it, superseding the paused Alacritty-sidecar implementation of [#1489](https://github.com/yicheng47/runner/issues/1489) and unblocking [#1492](https://github.com/yicheng47/runner/issues/1492) (`terminal-browser`). Because this change touches `runner-terminal`, `runner-core` (attach snapshots and resize frames), `runner-daemon` (session output, resize and PTY boundary) and `runner-app` (terminal rendering, selection, search and input), implementation pull requests carry `core-review`, `risk-high` and `needs-live-test`.

Code references are to `main` at `47b59c77` (2026-11-27). Upstream references are Ghostty `vt-1.0.2` (`ghostty-org/ghostty`, `include/ghostty/vt/*.h`, MIT). Prior art for embedding it from Rust is herdr (Apache-2.0): [`herdrdev/herdr-app`](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr-app) (`crates/ghostty-sys`, `src/vt.rs`), a GPUI desktop app split like Runner, and [`herdrdev/herdr-core`](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr-core) (`crates/ghostty-vt`, `src/relay/graphics.rs`), the process that owns its terminals. Runner reads them for the build approach and writes its own code.

## Motivation

Runner embeds a vendored `alacritty_terminal` 0.26 (`vte` 0.15) under an accessor-only patch rule (`vendor/alacritty_terminal/RUNNER_PATCH.md`). Upstream Alacritty intentionally omits inline graphics and many modern VT extensions, so Runner has accumulated a growing stack of external byte pre-scanners and wrappers around `Term`: `OSC 7` cwd scanning, `DEC 2031` color-scheme scanning and DECRQM reply rewriting, `OSC 7501` and `OSC 133` scanning, Kitty keyboard reply masking, `TrackedProcessor` / `BoundaryScanner` for synchronized updates and `REP`, and `find::ScrollTracker` to observe grid shifts outside `Term`.

Implementing [#1489](https://github.com/yicheng47/runner/issues/1489) on top of `alacritty_terminal` hit two structural walls at once:

- **Re-implementing Ghostty outside Alacritty.** Adding Kitty graphics, `CSI 16 t`, `DECSET 1016`, synchronized-update flushing/reopening and manual scroll/resize placement tracking outside `alacritty_terminal` required reverse-engineering Alacritty's private `Grid` behavior and duplicating Ghostty's graphics engine in Rust, while still leaving grapheme clustering (`?2027`), full Kitty keyboard modes and text sizing (`OSC 66`) unaddressed — even though `runner-ios` already runs Ghostty (`libghostty`).
- **Pushing 30 fps megapixel video through a base64 VT byte stream into a sprite atlas.** Normalizing `t=s` / `t=t` graphics back into inline `t=d` base64 frames over `runnerd.sock` inflated a 7.68 MB Retina frame to ~10.2 MB of ASCII (~300 MB/s at 30 fps), double-decoded images in daemon and mirror, split synthetic deletes from >1 MiB transmissions across frames and thrashed GPUI's `MetalAtlas` (`metal_atlas.rs:30-39`, `:134-205`), causing intermittent blank panes on macOS live QA (`PR #1495`).

Migrating `runner-terminal` to `libghostty-vt` (`vt-1.0.2`) removes `alacritty_terminal` and `vte` completely, unifies desktop, `runnerd` and `runner-ios` on one VT engine, deletes the external pre-scanners, introduces native binary attach snapshots and gives Runner full Kitty graphics (including `U=1` Unicode placeholders and animations), `OSC 7501`, `OSC 133`, `DEC 2031`, `DECSET 1016`, `CSI 14 t`/`16 t`, full Kitty keyboard, `?2027` grapheme clustering and `OSC 66` upstream. Pairing it with a local `t=f` raw-RGBA binary handoff and `window.paint_surface` / post-swap GPUI rendering solves the 30 fps streaming bottleneck cleanly.

## Current behavior

- **Vendored Alacritty model and leaked types.** `Cargo.toml` patches `alacritty_terminal` to `vendor/alacritty_terminal` (0.26.0 + read-only `SnapshotState`). Outside `crates/runner-terminal`, `alacritty_terminal` types leak into `runner-app` (`app_settings.rs:50`, `surfaces/settings/page/appearance.rs:837`, `surfaces/files/syntax.rs:387-393`, `terminal/{element,find,input}.rs`) and `runner-daemon` (`shell_integration.rs:618` and session tests).
- **External pre-scanners around `vte`.** `runner-terminal` scans raw bytes outside `vte` for `DEC 2031` (`terminal.rs:106-172`), `OSC 7` (`terminal.rs:177-273`), `OSC 7501` and `OSC 133` (`program_status.rs`), masks `CSI ? u` down to `DISAMBIGUATE_ESC_CODES` (`terminal.rs:119-125`), wraps `Term` in `find::ScrollTracker` (`terminal/find.rs:266`) and shadows `vte`'s `Processor` with `TrackedProcessor` and `BoundaryScanner` (`snapshot.rs:14-81`, `:396`).
- **Dual-thread `Term` ownership with `FairMutex`.** `TerminalModel` (`terminal/model.rs:49-334`) holds `Arc<FairMutex<Term<EventProxy>>>` and spawns two background threads per session (`native-term-events-*` and `native-term-sync-*`). `TerminalMirror` (`terminal.rs:434-730`) holds its own `Arc<FairMutex<Term<EventProxy>>>` and spawns two threads (`native-term-frames-*` and `native-term-sync-*`); GPUI's `TerminalElement::prepaint` (`runner-app/src/terminal/element.rs:1112`) locks `Term` directly on the UI thread.
- **VT-replayed snapshots.** `snapshot::serialize_with_history` (`snapshot.rs:87-217`) serializes the primary and alternate grids, cursor, tabs, title stack, color overrides and mode bits back into ANSI escape bytes plus trailing unfinished bytes and `preceding_char` for `REP`. Both local attach (`runnerd.sock`) and companion attach (`runner-ios` / remote desktop) use this VT byte snapshot.
- **Zero pixel geometry and text-only frame pipeline.** `PtyRuntime` opens and resizes PTYs with `pixel_width: 0, pixel_height: 0` (`session/pty_runtime.rs:222-223`, `:534-535`), and `Event::TextAreaSizeRequest` replies with `cell_width: 0, cell_height: 0` (`terminal/model.rs:245-256`). `ingest_output_for_launch` (`session/manager/output.rs:1334-1377`) publishes raw PTY bursts to every subscriber's `FrameQueue` (`FRAME_CAPACITY = 64`, `session/manager/terminal.rs:15`) before feeding `TerminalModel`, and `TerminalBridge` attaches a live mirror to every live session even when hidden.
- **GPUI sprite-atlas painting.** `TerminalElement` paints quads, procedural box-drawing glyphs and shaped text runs. Existing image rendering in Files (`surfaces/files/image.rs:837-863`) uploads BGRA `Arc<RenderImage>` tiles into GPUI's `MetalAtlas` (`paint_image`, `gpui-pre-0.3.14/src/window.rs:5547`) and releases them via `cx.drop_image`. `gpui-pre-0.3.14` also exposes `window.paint_surface(bounds, CFRetained<CVPixelBuffer>)` on macOS (`window.rs:5649`).

## Scope

**Phase 1 — Replace `alacritty_terminal` with `libghostty-vt` (`vt-1.0.2`) at behavioral parity:**

- Integrate `libghostty-vt` (`vt-1.0.2`, static Zig `0.17.0` build) behind `crates/runner-terminal` across macOS (`aarch64`, `x86_64`), Windows (`x86_64-pc-windows-msvc`) and Linux (`x86_64`, `aarch64`), with offline-safe source/cache wiring for local worktrees and CI.
- Remove `vendor/alacritty_terminal`, `alacritty_terminal` and `vte` from the workspace, replacing all leaked types with `runner-terminal`'s own public types.
- Reimplement `TerminalModel`, `TerminalMirror`, `input_state`, `delivery_probe`, `find`, `links`, `selection`, `palette` and `mappings` on `libghostty-vt` (`terminal.h`, `render.h`, `search.h`, `selection.h`, `key.h`, `mouse.h`, `paste.h`, `color_scheme.h`, `size_report.h`), keeping Kitty graphics storage disabled (`limit = 0`) in Phase 1 so the engine swap is verified cleanly on its own.
- Replace external pre-scanners (`CwdReports`, `SchemeState`, `program_status` byte parser, `ScrollTracker`, `BoundaryScanner`, `TrackedProcessor`, `kitty_flags_reply`) with `libghostty-vt`'s native effect callbacks (`on_pwd_changed`, `on_color_scheme`, `on_program_status`, `on_semantic_prompt`, `on_clipboard_write`, `on_size`, `on_render_hold`, `on_pty_write`), preserving full `OSC 7501` record-tree and `ActivityFilter` semantics. The `on_*` names in this spec are the Runner wrapper's handlers for the matching `GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_OPT_*` callback options (`WRITE_PTY`, `PWD_CHANGED`, `COLOR_SCHEME`, `CLIPBOARD_WRITE`, `SIZE`, `RENDER_HOLD`, `SEMANTIC_PROMPT`, `PROGRAM_STATUS`).
- Switch local desktop attach snapshots to native binary `ghostty_snapshot_encode` / `Decoder`, while keeping `Formatter` (`Format::Vt`) for companion snapshots so existing `runner-ios` builds keep connecting without protocol breakage.
- Update `TerminalElement` in `runner-app` to paint from `runner-terminal`'s `ViewportSnapshot` (derived from `libghostty-vt`'s `RenderState`), preserving procedural box-drawing glyphs, Retina sharpness, IME composition, selection, Cmd+F find and clickable file/URL links.

**Phase 2 — Kitty graphics, local `t=f` binary handoff, pixel geometry, SGR-Pixels mouse and 30 fps GPUI rendering (supersedes Alacritty `#1489`, unblocks `#1492`):**

- Enable `libghostty-vt`'s Kitty graphics, with a PNG decoder installed through `sys.h`, including `a=q/t/T/p/d`, `f=24/32/100`, `o=z`, `t=d/f/t/s`, relative placements, z-ordering, `U=1` Unicode placeholders and `a=f`/`a=a`/`a=c` animations (with visibility-gated `tick_animation`).
- Implement daemon-only ingestion of `t=t`/`t=s`/`t=f`/`t=d` with local daemon-managed `t=f` raw-RGBA file handoff (~80 bytes on `runnerd.sock` per frame instead of base64) and visibility-gated graphics for hidden tabs.
- Propagate device-pixel cell geometry (`cell_width`, `cell_height`) end-to-end across resize frames, snapshots, `PtySize` (`TIOCGWINSZ`), `CSI 14 t`, `CSI 16 t` and companion messages.
- Enable `DECSET 1016` (SGR-Pixels, zero-based as in Kitty and Ghostty) and hover motion reporting without a pressed button under mode `1003`.
- Render Kitty graphics in GPUI `TerminalElement` across Kitty's three z-order bands with post-swap texture retirement (zero blanking on replacement) and a macOS `window.paint_surface` (`CVPixelBuffer`) streaming path for 30+ fps placements.
- Strip Kitty graphics commands from companion output and companion snapshots in Phase 2, keeping companion text sessions clean.

**Phase 3 — Companion graphics and optional binary snapshots:**

- Capability-negotiated `kittyGraphics` streaming for remote desktop and `runner-ios` with compression, size caps and frame-drop coalescing under relay/Tailscale backpressure.
- Optional binary `ghostty_snapshot_encode` adoption on the companion protocol once `runner-ios` supports it.

**Out of scope:**

- Launcher UI, **Settings → Launcher**, `TERMINAL_BROWSER_TERMINAL_SOCKET` and macOS `⌘` browser routing ([#1492](https://github.com/yicheng47/runner/issues/1492)).
- Replacing local `TerminalMirror` with a daemon-rendered IPC viewport protocol (see [Decisions](#decisions) 1).
- Sixel or iTerm2 (`OSC 1337`) image protocols, tmux DCS passthrough unwrapping, or bundling `terminal-browser` / any browser engine.
- Changing `#818` (`docs/features/818-terminal-round-trips.md`); `#818` is orthogonal and stays compatible with both phases.

## Decisions

1. **Keep dual `libghostty-vt` (daemon model + app mirror), and move local pixels out of base64.** A single `libghostty-vt` in `runnerd` for local sessions was considered and rejected: `runner-app` still has to embed `libghostty-vt` for remote desktop sessions (relay / Tailscale `#954`), so daemon-rendered local panes would force two separate terminal frontends, put 120 Hz scrollback, selection, Cmd+F search and wrapped-link hover behind daemon IPC, and require a custom binary `RenderState` wire protocol. Keeping `TerminalModel` in `runnerd` and `TerminalMirror` in `runner-app` keeps one shared UI/interaction codepath for local and remote windows, while Phase 2's local `t=f` binary handoff removes the base64 socket bottleneck.
2. **Runner owns its binding: a `ghostty-vt-sys` crate built from Ghostty `vt-1.0.2` with Zig `0.17.0`, as herdr does.** `crates/ghostty-vt-sys` builds the tagged source from its `build.rs` with `zig build -Demit-lib-vt -Demit-xcframework=false -Doptimize=ReleaseFast -Dtarget=<target>`, mapping each Rust target to an explicit Zig target so Zig never builds for the build machine's newest CPU. It links the static library only, so no `.dylib`, `.dll` or `.so` ships beside the app, and generates raw declarations from `ghostty/vt.h` with `bindgen` (`ghostty_.*`, `Ghostty.*`, `GHOSTTY_.*`). The safe wrapper lives in `runner-terminal` and covers what Runner uses, as herdr's do (about 830 lines in `herdr-core` and 3,000 in `herdr-app`). The community crate `uzaaft/libghostty-rs` was considered and not chosen: its crates.io release (`0.2.2`) predates `vt-1.0.2`, so Runner would pin a git commit of a third party's wrapper and wait on it for each upstream change, while its own binding pins the exact tag and can carry a tracked local patch, as herdr carries two (`vendor/libghostty-vt.patches.md`). How the tagged source reaches a fresh worktree without network access is settled in 1a: a vendored copy under `vendor/libghostty-vt`, as in herdr, or a git submodule, as in herdr-app, with Zig's package dependencies cached (`zig build --system <dir>`). PNG decoding and logging are callbacks the embedder installs through `sys.h`: Runner decodes PNGs with the `png` crate and routes logs into `tracing`.
3. **Strict crate boundary: zero `libghostty_vt` types outside `crates/runner-terminal`.** `runner-terminal` exports Runner-owned types (`Rgb`, `CursorShape`, `TerminalModeFlags`, `ViewportSnapshot`, `CellRun`, `SelectionKind`, `SearchState`, `KittyPlacementSnapshot`). `runner-app` and `runner-daemon` never import `libghostty_vt` or `libghostty_vt_sys` directly.
4. **Single-owner actor threads respect `!Send`/`!Sync` with no `unsafe impl Send`.** `libghostty-vt`'s `Terminal` and `RenderState` handles are `!Send` and `!Sync`. `TerminalModel` runs one single-owner VT actor thread plus one dedicated FIFO host-effects worker so blocking `TerminalHost` callbacks never stall `vt_write` or re-enter the VT actor while preserving strict event/reply order. `TerminalMirror` runs one single-owner actor thread with bounded non-blocking push delivery through `TerminalSubscription::install_sink` (collapsing frame or byte overflow to a single `Resync` without blocking the producer, prioritizing UI/`Shutdown` commands and elapsed deadlines ahead of buffered output, and avoiding a separate `native-term-frames-*` thread for production transports), lazy `RenderState` publishing gated on `viewers > 0` (zero `RenderState::update` / `ScreenData` allocation for hidden printable or control chunks), and clean shutdown/cancel/join in `Drop` (including fallback recv-based subscriptions).
5. **Local attach uses binary `ghostty_snapshot_encode`; companion attach keeps VT `Formatter`.** Local snapshots (`TerminalSnapshot`, `SnapshotFormat::GhosttyBinary`) use `libghostty-vt`'s native binary snapshot format (`snapshot.h`) with `retain_continuation = true` and `compress_history = true`, carrying primary and alternate screens, scrollback, modes, continuation bytes, an optional self-delimiting `"RNTS"` footer when the xterm title stack is non-empty, and (in Phase 2) Kitty images without base64 bloat. When snapshot encoding or continuation capture fails (for example, an unfinished OSC exceeding the continuation cap), the snapshot sets `unavailable = true` instead of emitting empty binary bytes; mirrors retain their prior good view without advancing `sequence.last`, `runnerd` triggers a `Resync` as soon as a snapshot becomes representable again (even if the parser is inside a new short escape sequence), and a corrupt local `Resync` schedules a bounded automatic retry. Companion snapshots (`runner-ios` and remote desktop) keep `companion::VERSION = 1`, all fields and legacy fixtures unchanged, defer unavailable snapshots without emitting empty `HostMessage::Snapshot` frames, and compose VT replay from `libghostty-vt`'s `Formatter` (`Format::Vt` with `unwrap: true` so soft-wrapped lines reflow on remote resize like the host): the primary screen with requested history, saved and active cursors and full bit-0-capped Kitty keyboard stack, followed by the alternate-screen switch and active screen (with its saved/active cursors and Kitty stack) when an alternate-screen program is open, plus continuation bytes and `REP` state.
6. **Phase 1 disables Kitty graphics storage (`limit = 0`).** Separating the VT engine migration (Phase 1) from Kitty graphics, pixel geometry, `1016` mouse and 30 fps GPUI rendering (Phase 2) isolates regressions: Phase 1 is verified purely against existing terminal, agent, IME, scrollback, find, link and remote tests before Phase 2 adds graphics.
7. **Phase 2 local graphics use daemon-managed `t=f` raw-RGBA file handoff.** `runnerd`'s `TerminalModel` enables `t=f`, `t=t` and `t=s` (`OPT_KITTY_IMAGE_MEDIUM_*`), reads/unlinks/decodes each incoming image once into `f=32` RGBA and writes an immutable user-private backing file (`0600`) in a daemon-owned temp directory keyed by `(session_id, image_id, generation)`. Local mirrors have `t=t` and `t=s` disabled and `t=f` enabled; `runnerd` rewrites transmissions to a single non-chunked ~80-byte `a=T` (or `a=t`/`a=f`) command with `t=f,f=32,s=W,v=H,i=ID,q=2;<path>` (preserving placement keys). This shrinks 30 fps Retina socket traffic from ~10.2 MB/frame to ~80 bytes/frame, eliminates double base64/PNG decoding and makes `i=` replacement atomic inside one `vt_write` call. Backing files stay alive across a short grace window (or mirror ack / eviction) so multiple local windows read safely without `ENOENT`, and are swept on replacement, eviction, session exit and daemon startup. #1489's spec now holds this design in detail and wins where the two differ: it keeps runnerd as the only reader and replier, and leaves the pixel handoff, shared memory per subscriber or one private file per image, to a measurement, because a 30 fps full-pane stream handed over as files writes about 240 MB/s to the SSD on macOS.
8. **Hidden tabs pay zero graphics bandwidth and zero mirror image memory.** In Phase 2, local mirrors advertise visibility (`viewers > 0`) to `runnerd` (or park graphics while hidden). While `viewers == 0`, `runnerd` omits graphics transmissions for that subscriber; when a pane opens or refocuses a hidden session whose graphics generation advanced, the mirror catches up via a single binary `ghostty_snapshot_encode` resync (or targeted image transfer), keeping background `terminal-browser` panes near zero overhead.
9. **Zero-blanking GPUI painting with `paint_surface` for macOS streaming.** Static and small images render via GPUI `paint_image` (`Arc<RenderImage>`), but an overwritten texture is retired via `cx.drop_image` **only after** the replacement frame has been painted (never eagerly during `a=d` or mid-decode), preventing `MetalAtlas::metal_texture` (`metal_atlas.rs:30-39`) from returning `None` and dropping frames. On macOS, full-pane high-fps streaming placements (e.g., repeated same-ID replacements at >5 Hz) paint through `window.paint_surface(bounds, CFRetained<CVPixelBuffer>)` (`gpui-pre-0.3.14/src/window.rs:5649`) backed by a small `CVPixelBuffer` ring buffer so GPUI stops allocating and destroying multi-MB `MTLTexture`s 30 times per second.
10. **Pause `#1489` on Alacritty; carry its verification bar into Phase 2.** `#1489`'s Alacritty implementation (`PR #1495`, kept on `backup/1489-alacritty-kitty` at `7b09eae1`) stays paused so no further work is spent porting Ghostty's graphics engine on top of `alacritty_terminal`. Every behavioral decision settled in `#1489` (`5e7fb0be`) — zero-based SGR-Pixels `1016`, device-pixel cell sizing, 3-band z-ordering, single-reply authority, companion stripping and the live QA matrix — carries directly into Phase 2.
11. **Reflow, right-margin tab, scrollback pages and tab copying follow Ghostty.** When a window narrows, rows that re-wrap stay on screen until the screen is full; alacritty pushes them into scrollback even with empty rows below, so `resize_reflows_wrapped_lines` changes with it (Jason, 2026-11-27). Right-margin `HT` (`\t`) with wrap pending stays on the full bottom row and wraps/scrolls once on the next printable character (Ghostty VT100 behavior). Positive `scrollback_lines` maps to Ghostty's whole-page scrollback (`SCROLLBACK_MAX_LINES = lines`, `SCROLLBACK_MAX_BYTES = usize::MAX`), while `0` disables history (`SCROLLBACK_MAX_BYTES = 0`). Ghostty expands tab cells to spaces when copying (including across soft wraps; preserving literal tabs is a possible future follow-up) and drops an orphan combining mark placed after a cursor move over an unwritten tab cell because no tab character is stored, while an explicitly written space retains its combining mark and `#1356`'s no-shaped-tab guarantee holds.
12. **Grapheme clustering (mode 2027) stays off in Phase 1**, matching alacritty and `vt-1.0.2`'s default, so parity is judged on its own. A follow-up turns it on with its own emoji and CJK checks, since it changes the width of sequences such as flags and family emoji (Jason, 2026-11-27).
13. **Kitty keyboard protocol stays capped to bit 0 (`DISAMBIGUATE_ESC_CODES`) in Phase 1.** Mode flags, `CSI ? u` query replies, key encoding, live stack entries, exported companion `Formatter` VT (`CSI = N;1u`) and `snapshot_state` all mask Kitty keyboard flags to bit 0 so advertised support matches Runner's Phase 1 encoder. Enabling higher Kitty keyboard flags is deferred past Phase 1.
14. **Device attributes, clipboard and CWD policy.** The typed `GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_OPT_DEVICE_ATTRIBUTES` callback replies to DA1 with `\x1b[?6c` (VT102, zero features), preserves Runner's established DA2 identity `\x1b[>0;2600;1c` (keeping feature detection for programs like `vim`), and accepts Ghostty's standard DA3 `\x1bP!|00000000\x1b\\` (zero unit ID) — each emitting one reply from `TerminalModel` and zero from mirrors. Clipboard uses stock `vt-1.0.2` handlers (OSC 52, OSC 1337 Copy, and OSC 5522 UTF-8 `text/plain` writes up to 1 MiB) with `CLIPBOARD_READ` unset so OSC 52 reads stay silent, `CSI ? 5522 $ p` reports `;0$y` and `OSC 5522;type=read` replies `EPERM`. `OSC 7` CWD reports require a `file://` URI and pass `host.is_local_host`.
15. **Narrow tracked vendor patch for xterm title push/pop (`CSI 22 t` / `CSI 23 t`).** `vt-1.0.2` already parses `CSI 22 t` (`title_push`) and `CSI 23 t` (`title_pop`) as no-op actions, so `UNKNOWN_SEQUENCE` never sees them. As an explicit exception to the build-only patch rule, Runner carries one narrow tracked patch in `vendor/libghostty-vt` (`vendor/libghostty-vt.patches.md`, Patch #2) that only exposes those two already-parsed actions as `GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_OPT_TITLE_PUSH` / `TITLE_POP` callbacks without altering parsing or native terminal state, to be removed when upstream Ghostty exposes title push/pop to embedders (and proposed upstream). Runner owns the bounded stack (depth 4,096, params `0` and `2`, ignoring `1`), restores popped titles via `GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_OPT_TITLE`, clears the stack on RIS and session-title rebind, carries it across binary snapshots via the optional `"RNTS"` footer, and keeps `TerminalModel` → `runnerd` session metadata → `TerminalMirror::title()` as the authoritative live title path (while also backing `title_parser.rs` with `libghostty-vt`).
16. **Narrow tracked vendor patch for native per-screen scroll-up row count and generation (`GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_DATA_SCROLL_UP_ROWS` / `SCREEN_GENERATION`).** Viewport holding and Cmd+F match tracking need exact per-screen counts of rows scrolled upward off row 0 across arbitrary VT writes — covering `LF` at bottom, `IND`, `NEL`, `SU` (`CSI n S`), `REP` (`CSI n b`), `DL` (`CSI n M`) at region top and `scrollClear`, including on zero-scrollback screens and across temporary alternate-screen excursions — without maintaining a Rust VT byte scanner. As a second narrow tracked exception (`vendor/libghostty-vt.patches.md`, Patch #3), Runner adds `scroll_up_rows: u64` and `scroll_epoch: u64` (incremented on in-place `Screen.reset()`) to `Screen`, exposed read-only via `GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_DATA_SCROLL_UP_ROWS = 43` alongside `GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_DATA_SCREEN_GENERATION = 44` (combining `ScreenSet.generation` with `Screen.scroll_epoch` without altering `ScreenSet.generations`, keeping `TrackedGridRef` pin untracking intact across `RIS`), removes the Rust scroll scanner entirely, preserves surviving primary scroll marks and held primary viewports across temporary alternate excursions regardless of chunk splits, retains `TrackedGridRef` in `runner-terminal::vt` for production match/viewport anchoring delivered on the Phase 1c gate, and proposes the counter/callback upstream to Ghostty for removal once upstream exposes it.

## Design

### Build and dependency wiring

- Add `crates/ghostty-vt-sys` (Decision 2) as `runner-terminal`'s only dependency on Ghostty, with `bindgen` as its build dependency, and `png` to `runner-terminal`. Remove `alacritty_terminal` from workspace `Cargo.toml` (`[patch.crates-io]`), `crates/runner-terminal/Cargo.toml`, `crates/runner-app/Cargo.toml` and `crates/runner-daemon/Cargo.toml`, and delete `vendor/alacritty_terminal/`.
- Install the PNG decoder and log callbacks (`sys.h`) once at `runner-terminal` startup (`std::sync::Once`) so `libghostty-vt` decodes `f=100` PNGs and routes internal diagnostics into Runner's `tracing` log.
- Require Zig `0.17.0` in dev and CI (matching Ghostty `vt-1.0.2`'s `build.zig.zon`). Add `mlugg/setup-zig` (`0.17.0`) plus a cached Zig package directory in `.github/workflows/{ci.yaml,nightly.yml,release.yml}`, covering all five targets (`aarch64-apple-darwin`, `x86_64-apple-darwin`, `x86_64-pc-windows-msvc`, Linux `x86_64` and `aarch64`). Add a matching local cache helper in `Makefile` / `script/` so fresh `.worktrees/` never clone GitHub during `cargo build`. The build script takes the Zig binary from `ZIG`, as herdr's does, so a machine can keep another Zig as its default. Windows is built first: herdr needed a local patch for Zig's MSVC libc headers in Wuffs (`vendor/libghostty-vt.patches.md`, patch 0004).

### Public `runner-terminal` boundary

`crates/runner-terminal` exposes pure Runner types so no downstream crate knows `libghostty-vt` exists:

- **Color & cursor types:** `palette::Rgb`, `CursorShape` (`Block`, `HollowBlock`, `Underline`, `Beam`, `Hidden`) and `TerminalModeFlags` (alt screen, cursor visible, bracketed paste, mouse reporting modes, SGR/SGR-Pixels, app cursor/keypad, focus reporting, Kitty keyboard flags).
- **Render snapshot:** `ViewportSnapshot` produced by `TerminalMirror` from `libghostty_vt::RenderState`, carrying `cols`, `rows`, `display_offset`, `history_rows`, resolved palette/defaults, cursor position/shape/visibility, selection range, active find match ranges, visible rows of cell runs (grapheme clusters, wide-char flags, style flags, fg/bg/underline colors, OSC 8 hyperlink URI) and (in Phase 2) visible Kitty placements sorted into the three z-bands with `(image_id, generation, source_rect, dest_grid_or_px, z)`.
- **Input encoders:** `mappings.rs` delegates key, paste and mouse encoding to `libghostty-vt`'s `KeyEncoder`, `MouseEncoder` and `paste` helpers (preserving Runner's `classify_key` for `InputTracker` and macOS Option-as-Meta / layout-aware rules).

### Single-owner actor threading and GPUI painting

- **`TerminalModel` (in `runnerd`):** One single-owner VT actor thread owns `libghostty_vt::Terminal` plus `InputTracker`, `program_status` record state and fixture recorder; a dedicated FIFO host-effects worker dispatches `TerminalHost` callbacks off the VT thread so blocking host work never stalls `vt_write` or re-enters the actor while preserving strict event and reply order. Commands (`FeedOutput`, `Resize`, `ObserveInput`, `WithUnchangedInput`, `SnapshotBinary`, `SnapshotVt`, `SetPalette`, `Configure`, `SyncSessionTitle`, `Shutdown`) execute sequentially in arrival order. Effect callbacks registered on the `Terminal` (`on_pty_write`, `on_title_changed`, `on_title_push`, `on_title_pop`, `on_pwd_changed`, `on_clipboard_write`, `on_program_status`, `on_semantic_prompt`, `on_color_scheme`, `on_size`, `on_device_attributes`) run synchronously inside `vt_write` and enqueue host replies/notifications onto the FIFO worker. Synchronized-update holds (`on_render_hold`) arm the actor's 150 ms deadline via `recv_timeout` on the same thread — eliminating the separate `native-term-sync-*` thread.
- **`TerminalMirror` (in `runner-app`):** One single-owner actor thread owns the mirror's `libghostty_vt::Terminal`, `RenderState`, `Search` and `Selection`. Production transports (`SocketTransport`, `SessionManager::Frames`, `remote::client::terminal::Frames`) deliver `TerminalFrame`s directly into the actor's command queue via `TerminalSubscription::install_sink`, eliminating the separate `native-term-frames-*` thread; `Drop` sends `Shutdown` and joins the actor thread cleanly. Replies (`on_pty_write`) are discarded on mirrors, preserving `TERM-QUERY-01`. When a DEC 2026 hold begins mid-chunk, `on_render_hold(true)` synchronously snapshots the pre-hold frame so output preceding `CSI ? 2026 h` remains visible while held. After applying each output batch (held while `on_render_hold` is active, woken on release or 150 ms timeout), and only while `viewers > 0`, the actor refreshes `RenderState`, builds a new `Arc<ViewportSnapshot>`, swaps it into a shared slot and calls `waker()`; hidden mirrors (`viewers == 0`) defer `RenderState` work until `view()` transitions `0 → 1` or `viewport_snapshot()` requests a refresh.
- **`TerminalElement::prepaint` (GPUI UI thread):** Reads the latest `Arc<ViewportSnapshot>` without ever blocking on `vt_write`. Cell shaping, procedural box drawing (`glyphs.rs`), hover tooltips, IME marked text and selection/find quads run over the snapshot exactly as today.

### Snapshots and companion compatibility

- **Local attach (`TerminalSnapshot`):** `TerminalSnapshot` carries `format: SnapshotFormat` (`Vt` or `GhosttyBinary`, defaulting to `Vt` for legacy fixtures/companion) and `unavailable: bool` (defaulting to `false`, encoded on `runnerd.sock` in the snapshot header format byte). Local `runnerd` ↔ `runner-app` attach uses `SnapshotFormat::GhosttyBinary` produced by `terminal.encode_snapshot(...)` (with `continuation_max_bytes` enabled so split escape sequences survive attach natively, deleting `BoundaryScanner`, `TrackedProcessor` and `preceding_char` on the binary path, and appending a self-delimiting `"RNTS"` footer only when the title stack is non-empty). When encoding or continuation capture fails, the snapshot carries `unavailable: true` instead of empty bytes; `TerminalMirror` retains its last good view without advancing `sequence.last`, and `runnerd` schedules a `Resync` once output returns the parser to ground. On attach or `Resync`, `TerminalMirror` decodes binary snapshots via `snapshot::Decoder` (`set_retain_continuation(true)`, `set_compress_history(true)`).
- **Companion attach (`runner-ios` and remote desktop):** `attach_companion_terminal` requests `SnapshotFormat::Vt`, composed from `libghostty-vt`'s `Formatter` (`Format::Vt`, `unwrap: true`, with palette, modes, scrolling region, tabstops, pwd, keyboard, cursor, style, hyperlink, Kitty keyboard capped to bit 0, and charsets enabled). When the alternate screen is active, the replay emits the primary screen and requested history first, then switches to the alternate screen and emits its contents, followed by title stack, active title, continuation bytes and `REP` state. `companion::VERSION = 1`, all fields and legacy fixtures stay unchanged so existing TestFlight `runner-ios` clients keep working without protocol breakage.

### Native callbacks, `OSC 7501`, selection, search, links and `InputTracker`

- **Callbacks replacing scanners:**
  - `on_pwd_changed` replaces `CwdReports` (`OSC 7`, still checking `host.is_local_host` and raising metadata change on directory change for `#818`).
  - `on_color_scheme` + `color_scheme_report_encode` replace `SchemeState`, `SCHEME_SUBSCRIBE`/`SCHEME_QUERY` scanning and `SCHEME_PROBE_UNSUPPORTED` DECRQM rewriting (`DEC 2031`).
  - `on_clipboard_write` replaces `Event::ClipboardStore` (stock `vt-1.0.2` handles OSC 52, OSC 1337 Copy and OSC 5522 UTF-8 `text/plain` writes up to 1 MiB, with `CLIPBOARD_READ` unset so reads are denied).
  - `on_size` + `size_report_encode` replace `Event::TextAreaSizeRequest` (`CSI 14 t`, `CSI 16 t`, `CSI 18 t`), computing in `u32` without Alacritty's `u16` overflow, and `on_device_attributes` serves DA1 (`\x1b[?6c`), DA2 (`\x1b[>0;2600;1c`) and DA3 (`\x1bP!|00000000\x1b\\`).
  - `on_program_status` + `on_semantic_prompt` replace the byte-level OSC parser in `program_status.rs` for `OSC 7501` and `OSC 133`. Runner retains its own record-tree/child-count rollup, `D→A` shell-return clear guard and raw-byte `ActivityFilter` (matching Ghostty's VT500 ESC-ends-OSC and CAN/SUB abort semantics byte-for-byte, and clearing post-invalidation discard at the native OSC/ground boundary regardless of PTY chunk splits).
  - `kitty_flags_reply` byte scanning is removed; `libghostty-vt` owns the Kitty keyboard stack natively, clamped to bit 0 (`DISAMBIGUATE_ESC_CODES`) across live state, replies, key encoding, companion VT export and `snapshot_state` in Phase 1 (Decision 13).
- **Search, selection, links and `InputTracker`:**
  - `terminal/find.rs` migrates from `alacritty_terminal` grid walking + `ScrollTracker` to `libghostty-vt`'s `search.h` (`GhosttySearch`) and `grid_ref_tracked.h` so selected matches track scrollback natively.
  - Selection migrates to `selection.h` (`GhosttySelection` / `GhosttySelectionGesture` for cell/word/line/block selection and `Formatter` with `with_selection` for exact copy text across soft wraps).
  - `terminal/links.rs`, `input_state.rs` and `delivery_probe.rs` read visible rows/styles/hyperlinks from `runner-terminal`'s `ViewportSnapshot` / `Terminal` grid accessors (`grid_ref` / `RenderState`), preserving every existing fixture replay test (`tests/fixture_replay.rs`, `tests/input_state_replay.rs`, `tests/delivery_probe_replay.rs`).

### Phase 2: Kitty graphics, local `t=f` handoff, pixel geometry, `1016` mouse and GPUI rendering

#1489's spec (`docs/features/1489-kitty-graphics-protocol.md` on `feat/1489-kitty-graphics-protocol`) holds Phase 2 in detail and wins where the two differ; the bullets below summarize it.

- **Pixel geometry and `DECSET 1016` / `1003`:** Carry `cell_width` and `cell_height` (device pixels, `em_advance * scale_factor` and `row_height * scale_factor`, rounded, clamped 1..=1024) through `TerminalGridSize`, `RESIZE`, `RESIZED`, `TerminalSnapshot`, `terminal_take_back`, `PtySize` (`TIOCGWINSZ`, skipping ConPTY resize on cell-size-only changes on Windows), `Terminal::resize(cols, rows, cell_w, cell_h)` and additive companion `cellWidth`/`cellHeight` fields. `libghostty-vt` handles `CSI 14 t`, `CSI 16 t`, `DECSET 1016` (zero-based) and `DECRQM 1016` natively; `TerminalElement` enables unpressed motion reporting under mode `1003` while the pane hitbox is hovered and tracks pixel coordinates in `DragKind::Reported` under `1016`.
- **Daemon-side ingestion and local `t=f` handoff:**
  - `runnerd`'s `TerminalModel` enables `set_kitty_image_storage_limit(Some(320_000_000))` per screen (plus a 1 GiB atomic daemon-wide guard across sessions), `set_kitty_image_from_file_allowed(true)`, `set_kitty_image_temp_file_dir(Some(tmp))` and `set_kitty_image_from_shared_mem_allowed(true)` (Unix).
  - **Fast path (no `\x1b_G` in burst and scanner idle):** raw burst publishes straight to subscribers unchanged, keeping zero overhead for ordinary agent/shell output.
  - **Graphics path (burst contains `\x1b_G`):** a thin APC framer splits the burst into text segments and complete Kitty APC packets, feeding each into `runnerd`'s `TerminalModel`. `libghostty-vt` performs all parsing, `t=f`/`t=t`/`t=s` reading/unlinking, PNG/zlib decoding, cursor moves, eviction and replies (`on_pty_write`). When a transmission completes and advances an image's `(id, generation)`, `runnerd` writes the decoded `f=32` RGBA bytes once to `$TMPDIR/runner-gfx/<session_id>/<id>-<gen>.rgba` (mode `0600`) and emits a single non-chunked ~80-byte `a=T` / `a=t` / `a=f` command with `t=f,f=32,s=W,v=H,i=ID,q=2;<path>` (preserving placement/frame keys) to visible local mirrors. Placement (`a=p`), delete (`a=d`) and animation (`a=a`/`a=c`) commands forward as small commands with `q=2`; queries (`a=q`) and failed commands are omitted; companion subscribers receive text segments only.
  - Backing files are kept alive until replaced/evicted plus a short grace period (so concurrent local windows never race on `read`), then unlinked; a session's directory is removed on exit and stale directories are pruned on `runnerd` startup.
- **Visibility gating for hidden tabs:** `TerminalMirror` notifies `runnerd` when its viewer count transitions `0 ↔ >0`. While a subscriber has zero viewers, `runnerd` skips emitting graphics commands for it and marks its graphics dirty; on the `0 → 1` transition, if graphics changed while hidden, `runnerd` triggers a single binary snapshot resync so the pane displays the latest image immediately without streaming 30 fps frames into hidden tabs.
- **GPUI rendering and zero-blanking lifecycle:**
  - `TerminalElement::paint` draws Kitty placements from `ViewportSnapshot` in Kitty's three z-order bands (`z < i32::MIN / 2`, `i32::MIN / 2 <= z < 0`, `z >= 0` below cursor/IME, clipped to the pane's content mask), reading decoded RGBA pixels by `(image_id, generation)` from the mirror's `libghostty-vt` image cache.
  - **Static / low-fps placements:** upload BGRA `Arc<RenderImage>` (downscaled to <=8,192 px per side) and paint with `window.paint_image(bounds, image_bounds, ...)` for zero-copy source cropping. Retain the previous `Arc<RenderImage>` for an overwritten `(image_id)` until **after** the frame that paints the replacement has been submitted, then call `cx.drop_image`; this eliminates blank frames caused by eager `MetalAtlas` eviction (`metal_atlas.rs:30-39`).
  - **Streaming placements (macOS):** when a placement's image updates repeatedly (>5 Hz, e.g., `terminal-browser`, `krd`, `rex-sim`), copy BGRA pixels into a pooled `CVPixelBuffer` (`IOSurface`-backed, 2–3 buffers in flight) and paint via `window.paint_surface(bounds, pixel_buffer)` (`gpui-pre-0.3.14/src/window.rs:5649`), bypassing `MetalAtlas` allocation churn completely. Non-macOS platforms use the post-swap `RenderImage` path until a WGPU/DirectX streaming texture path is needed.
  - **Animations:** `TerminalMirror`'s actor runs `graphics.tick_animation(now_ms)` only while `viewers > 0` and the window is visible, scheduling its next wake at the returned `next_ms` and stopping completely when hidden.

### Open questions to settle before Phase 1 kickoff

1. **How the pinned source reaches a worktree (settled by the 1a trial):** Vendor Ghostty's official `libghostty-vt-1.0.2.tar.gz` source tarball verbatim under `vendor/libghostty-vt/` (25 MB unpacked / 4.55 MB compressed, smaller than the 46 MB `vendor/alacritty_terminal` it replaces), tracked by `vendor/libghostty-vt.vendor.json` (version, tag, commit, URLs and SHA-256 digests) and `vendor/libghostty-vt.patches.md`. Keep Zig's 5 external packages (`aro`, `highway`, `translate_c`, `uucode`, `wuffs`) out of git in a shared per-user offline `--system` directory (`${XDG_CACHE_HOME:-$HOME/.cache}/runner/libghostty-vt-deps/1.0.2-c56156db` on Unix, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\runner\libghostty-vt-deps\1.0.2-c56156db` on Windows, overridable via `LIBGHOSTTY_VT_ZIG_SYSTEM_DIR`), populated once from Ghostty's official SHA-256-verified `libghostty-vt-1.0.2-deps.tar.gz` (5.6 MB) by `script/fetch-libghostty-vt-deps.py` (`make ghostty-deps`) and cached in CI with `actions/cache`. Vendoring beat a git submodule because `git worktree add` leaves submodules unpopulated and would require cloning the 144 MB Ghostty repo (or custom `--reference` plumbing) per worktree, and submodules cannot carry tracked local patches cleanly without maintaining a fork; with vendored source, the shared `--system` directory, and an archive-free Zig build cache isolated under each worktree's `target/<profile>/ghostty-vt-zig-cache/`, every fresh `.worktrees/` checkout builds offline with zero submodule steps and zero cross-worktree lock contention.
2. **Exact `OSC 7501` parity check (settled in 1b):** Verified against `docs/features/archive/861-program-status.md` (`vt::tests::osc_7501_parity_spike_against_861_rules`). `libghostty-vt`'s native `GHOSTTY_TERMINAL_OPT_PROGRAM_STATUS` answers `ESC ] 7501 ; ? ST` with the full probe reply and enforces the `#861` byte/segment/id rules natively, with VT500 ESC-ends-OSC and CAN/SUB abort semantics mirrored byte-for-byte in `ActivityFilter`; post-invalidation discard stops at the native OSC/ground boundary even when a new record follows in the same PTY chunk.
3. **Actor latency vs. thread count at scale (settled in 1b):** Measured in `--release` across 25 open 120×40 sessions (`benchmark_25_open_sessions_memory_threads_and_latency` on Apple Silicon macOS): baseline 7.02 MiB / 2 threads → 25 idle sessions +21.08 MiB (0.84 MiB/session) and +75 threads (3 per session: 2 for `TerminalModel`, 1 for `TerminalMirror`) → 25 populated sessions (2,000 styled lines each, `compress_history = true`) +128.19 MiB / +75 threads → post-shutdown 2 threads (zero leaks). Latencies: `model_feed` p50 152.0 µs / p99 172.1 µs; hidden mirror (`viewers == 0`, lazy `RenderState` and lazy visible-text hashing) p50 5.2 µs / p99 15.4 µs; visible mirror (`viewers == 1`) p50 225.1 µs / p99 254.4 µs; `viewport_snapshot()` accessor (mutex-protected `Arc::clone` of the published snapshot, without blocking on `vt_write`; actual GPUI paint-path validation stays on the 1c gate) p50 41 ns / p99 83 ns; binary snapshot average 446 KB (encode p50 126.2 µs, decode p50 699.0 µs). Because hidden mirrors cost 5.2 µs per burst and 0.84 MiB idle, parking hidden `Terminal` instances is not needed in Phase 1; Phase 2 keeps visibility-gated graphics.
4. **Windows `t=f` and ConPTY APC pass-through:** Verify on the Windows PC in Phase 2 whether ConPTY passes `ESC _ G ... ESC \` through intact and whether `libghostty-vt`'s `t=f` path checks accept daemon-owned temp files under `%TEMP%\runner-gfx\`.
5. **Who handles graphics, runnerd or the viewers:** `herdr-core` relays a program's bytes unchanged, lets each viewer's own engine handle graphics, and matches Kitty replies to the viewer that sends them (`src/relay/graphics.rs`), while Decision 7 makes runnerd the only reader and replier. Compare the two before 2a, in particular for `t=t` and `t=s`, which the first reader deletes, and for a session with no viewer. Settled in #1489's Decision 1: runnerd stays the only reader and replier, because a session with no window open must still answer queries and read its media.

## Implementation Phases

1. **1a — Build pipeline and `runner-terminal` boundary:** Build `ghostty-vt-sys` (`vt-1.0.2`, Zig `0.17.0`, offline cache) and wire it into CI (Windows x64 first, then macOS arm64/x64 and Linux arm64/x64); define Runner-owned terminal types (`Rgb`, `CursorShape`, `TerminalModeFlags`, `ViewportSnapshot`) and remove leaked `alacritty_terminal` imports from `runner-app` and `runner-daemon`.
2. **1b — `TerminalModel`, `TerminalMirror`, snapshots and callbacks (`runner-terminal`, `runner-core`, `runner-daemon`):** Port `TerminalModel` and `TerminalMirror` to single-owner `libghostty-vt` actors; wire native effect callbacks (`OSC 7`, `DEC 2031`, `OSC 52`, `OSC 7501`, `OSC 133`, `CSI 14 t`/`16 t`, `2026` render hold); switch local `TerminalSnapshot` to `ghostty_snapshot_encode` / `Decoder` and companion snapshots to `Formatter` (`Format::Vt`); port `input_state`, `delivery_probe`, `mappings` and `replay` and pass the entire existing `runner-terminal` fixture corpus.
3. **1c — GPUI rendering, selection, search, anchoring and Alacritty removal (`runner-app`):** Paint `TerminalElement` from `ViewportSnapshot`; wire `selection.h`, `search.h`, `links.rs`, and `TrackedGridRef` production match/viewport anchoring; delete `vendor/alacritty_terminal`, `alacritty_terminal` and `vte` from `Cargo.toml`/`Cargo.lock`; run full terminal regression QA on macOS (plus CI on Windows and Linux).
4. **2a — Pixel geometry, `DECSET 1016` / `1003`, Kitty graphics and local `t=f` handoff (`runner-terminal`, `runner-core`, `runner-daemon`):** Propagate `(cell_width, cell_height)` end to end; enable Kitty graphics (`limit = 320 MiB` per screen, `1 GiB` daemon cap, `U=1` placeholders and animations); add the daemon APC framer, local `t=f` raw-RGBA backing-file handoff, visibility gating and companion stripping.
5. **2b — GPUI 3-band image painting and 30 fps streaming (`runner-app`):** Render Kitty placements in three z-bands with post-swap `drop_image` retirement and macOS `window.paint_surface` (`CVPixelBuffer`) streaming; wire visibility-gated `tick_animation`; run the 30 fps streaming, `terminal-browser`, Retina/1×, scrollback, Keep/reattach and remote-stripping live QA suite.
6. **3 — Companion graphics and optional binary snapshots (follow-up PR + `runner-ios`):** Capability-negotiated `kittyGraphics` streaming over the companion protocol with compression, budgets and frame-drop coalescing.

1a opens with a trial that decides whether Phase 1 goes ahead: the binding builds on every CI target, and the fixture corpus (`tests/fixture_replay.rs` and its recorded fixtures) is replayed into `libghostty-vt` and compared with the blessed screens. If the build or the corpus shows a gap the trial cannot close, the migration stops and #1489 resumes from `backup/1489-alacritty-kitty`.

The trial ran on 2026-11-27 and passed. `ghostty-vt-sys` built and linked statically on macOS arm64 and x86_64, Windows x64 and Linux x86_64 and arm64 against `vt-1.0.2`, with no patches to Ghostty. All 24 recorded fixtures replayed with screens identical to their blessed snapshots, cursor included. Of the 11 focused cases, 10 matched; in `resize_reflows_wrapped_lines`, alacritty pushes re-wrapped rows into scrollback when a window narrows, while Ghostty keeps them on screen until the screen overflows. The comparison covered text, cursor, wide characters and modes, not colors or styles, which `input_state_replay` and `delivery_probe_replay` exercise in 1b.

Before 1a–1c, a refactor that changes no behavior lands on `main` (`refactor/1508-terminal-boundary`): `runner-terminal`'s public API and every caller outside it move onto Runner-owned types and one read-only screen view, and `alacritty_terminal` and `vte` leave `runner-app`'s and `runner-daemon`'s dependencies. After it, 1a–1c change only `runner-terminal` and the build.

Phase 1 (1a–1c) lands as its own milestone PR (deleting `alacritty_terminal` completely before turning on graphics). Phase 2 (2a–2b) lands next and closes `#1489` / unblocks `#1492`.

### Soak before merge

Phase 1 merges only after a soak on the Preview Nightly channel, which exists for testing a branch before it reaches `main`:

1. Once Phase 1 has passed review and its live checks, the operator merges `feat/1508-libghostty-vt` into `preview`, and every push to `preview` that changes more than docs cuts an all-platform Preview Nightly.
2. Jason uses the Preview Nightly as his only Runner for at least a week, or until every item below is covered, whichever is later. A codex `qa` worker works through the same list in the branch's development build. Findings go back to the crew, and each fix pushed to the branch cuts a new build.
3. The pull request that lands Phase 1 needs no list change; `preview` picks the landing up when it next merges `main`. The next stable release then waits for two to three days of the regular nightly.

Soak checklist:

- **Agent status:** Claude Code, Codex, pi and Grok each show Working, Blocked and Done correctly through a real task.
- **Mission message delivery:** messages into agents are confirmed delivered, with no duplicate submits and no stuck unsent notices.
- **Input:** composer detection, Pinyin IME composition, paste, Option+arrow word movement and pi's Kitty keyboard.
- **Text:** CJK and emoji widths, box drawing, and full-screen programs entering and leaving the alternate screen.
- **Scrollback, selection and find:** copying across wrapped lines, and Cmd+F while output streams.
- **Keep, quit and reopen** with long histories.
- **Remote windows and companion clients** attached to the same sessions.
- **CPU** when idle and with an agent's spinner running.
- **Windows and Linux:** at least a day on each.
