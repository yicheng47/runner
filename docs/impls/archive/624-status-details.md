## Decisions that bind

1. **The label carries the detail.** The pane header, the single-pane tab bar and the runner card subtitle show the same short form from one function (`Idle · Interrupted`, `Approval needed · 2m`, `Working · Using tools`, `Working · Compacting context`); no tooltip where that label is visible; a glyph-only indicator (narrow header, sidebar row) keeps the tooltip as its name, with the seconds and the concurrent conditions. The label slot reserves its width so a toggling detail never shifts the title; too narrow drops the detail first, then the label goes icon-only as in 347. Sidebar rows stay glyph-only; the one sidebar change is the failure glyph. This replaces the first version of this decision ("nothing new in a label", 347's tooltip-only rule): Jason overturned it on 2026-09-17 after testing the first build, because nobody hovers a header; after the second build he dropped the tooltip that duplicated the label.
2. **Rollups count, they never time or name a detail.** `1 approval needed · 1 working` stays exactly that. A single-pane row is the pane, so its tooltip is the pane's tooltip with a masked unread response appended as a second clause; when the unread dot is the dominant glyph the row keeps 347's `1 unread response`.
3. **`failed_since` mirrors `error_since`.** Set on the running session when a `Failed` outcome arrives, cleared when the pane is viewed and when the outcome clears, counted by rollups as `response failed` next to `error`, ranked with error. Not persisted: a live-process outcome does not survive a restart today and still does not. The pane's own label is still read from the outcome and is untouched by acknowledgement.
4. **Elapsed format is fixed:** seconds under a minute, whole minutes under an hour, then hours and minutes (`45s`, `2m`, `1h 12m`). The tooltip refreshes every second while shown; the card shows minutes only, nothing under a minute, and redraws on the minute. Time never changes colour, precedence, ordering or delivery.
5. **The working detail is a hook fact.** `Using tools` spans `PreToolUse` to the owning result with the ownership the waits already use; `Compacting context` spans `PreCompact` to `PostCompact`, or on Copilot to the next work event or `Stop`; compaction outranks a tool in flight. A manual `/compact` from Idle fires no Stop, so each adapter decides at `PreCompact` whether a turn was live, keeps that across the compact-sourced `SessionStart`, and at the end resumes Working only for an in-turn compaction, otherwise restores Ready with the previous outcome (review finding, 2026-09-17). Baseline Working never carries a detail, enforced at the observation publish chokepoint. Claude Code registers the two compaction hooks; the reporter and feed are unchanged.
6. **The nonblocking ask is cut.** No hook publishes the flag (Codex exposes it only over app-server, and Codex has no Answer needed state anyway). It stays in the 347 "unsupported until observable" list.
7. **One mission, one PR.** All four items land together: the three UI-only ones in `agent_status.rs` and its consumers plus `failed_since`, and the working detail in the observation, the three adapters and Claude's hook registration. The mission stops at a clean working-tree review; Claude lands the PR after its own pass and green CI on both platforms. (Planned as two missions at first; Jason folded them on 2026-09-17 because the adapter change is small.)
