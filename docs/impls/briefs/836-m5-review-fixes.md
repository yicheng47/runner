Reproduction: attach and consume the initial result/snapshot/resized, produce more than 1 MiB in normal 8 KiB chunks ending in `FINAL-MARKER` without draining the browser, end the process and wait until it is no longer live, then drain the adapter. It returns `closed` without the snapshot containing the final marker. Preserve a bounded final state for the already attached stream and send it before closure; do not make stopped sessions generally attachable or retain terminals indefinitely. The host shows `abcccc`, while byte-only replay shows `abc`. Supply equivalent continuation for companion snapshots, including resync and unfinished parser state, without breaking native snapshot replay.
